## 2. Two New Mechanistic Insights Discovered During the Stress-Test

### Insight A: The Respiratory Clamp Loophole (Why Nose-Pinching Outperforms Reading)
Empirical practitioner and induction data consistently find that kinetic/respiratory checks—especially pinching one's nose and trying to breathe—are far more jarring and reliable than reading text. Why?
- During REM sleep, skeletal muscles are paralyzed (REM atonia), **but diaphragmatic respiration is spared**.
- When a dreamer pinches their *dream* nose, their *real physical chest and lungs* continue drawing air.
- That injects a genuine **non-zero physical sensory clamp ($\Pi_{\text{sens}} > 0$)** directly into the un-clamped dream model, creating an irreconcilable contradiction between the *visual prior* ("my nostrils are sealed") and the *actual proprioceptive/airflow afferent* ("air is flowing").

### Insight B: Dream Stabilization as Deliberate Prior-Flooding
When a dreamer becomes lucid, sudden emotional/cortical arousal risks collapsing REM and triggering waking sensory clamping ($\Pi_{\text{sens}} \to 1$). Empirical stabilization techniques—rubbing hands together, touching walls, spinning—work by **deliberately flooding the posterior renderer with high-precision endogenous tactile and vestibular predictions**, locking attention inside the internal generative loop before external sensory afferents can recapture the network.

## 3. Revision to Hypothesis v0.7.1

Incorporating the Aspy et al. (2017) falsification of naive anomaly-triggered lucidity yields **v0.7.1**:
1. **Renderer artifacts are necessary diagnostics, not automatic triggers:** Because non-lucid REM runs with $\gamma_{\text{meta}} \approx 0$, severe lazy-evaluation drift (`>85%` unobserved mutation) is routinely rationalized away unless frontopolar metacognitive precision is **pre-primed** (via WBTB/MILD or targeted REM cueing).
2. **Sensory disconnection is selectively porous, not absolute:** Even in deep REM, spared autonomic/respiratory afferents (and salience-gated acoustic channels; Sifuentes Ortega & Strauss, 2026, `10.1111/jsr.70404`) leak real physical signals across the boundary—providing both a natural reality-testing channel and a critical confound that any future THRESHOLD lab protocol must strictly control.
