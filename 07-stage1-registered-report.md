## Part 2: OSF Stage-1 Registered Report Manuscript

### 1. Title
**Adversarial Multi-Laboratory Test of State-Dependent Information Access During Real-Time Lucid REM Sleep (Project ONEIRIS: Closed-Loop THRESHOLD–ECHO Protocol)**

### 2. Abstract
Whether sensory-disconnected conscious states such as REM dreaming are wholly generated by endogenous neural models (*Model A*) or permit verifiable access to externally isolated information (*Model C / ONEIRIS v0.6*) remains empirically contested only when retrospective memory decay, sensory leakage, and flexible judging are left uncontrolled. Here we preregister a multi-laboratory adversarial collaboration ($\ge 3$ independent sleep laboratories) combining real-time two-way lucid REM communication via bilateral electrooculography (`LRLR` ocular signaling; Konkoly et al., 2021), TMS-EEG verification of reduced evoked alpha power ($8\text{–}12\text{ Hz}$; Darracq et al., 2018), and air-gapped hardware quantum random number generator (QRNG) + NIST Randomness Beacon cryptographic target commitment. By locking real-time intra-REM forced-choice responses ($R \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$) before unblinding across a preregistered sequential Bayes Factor Design Analysis (BFDA) and fixed-$N$ confirmatory cap, this protocol removes both morning-amnesia escape hatches and experimenter-cueing artifacts, providing a decisive falsification or confirmation test of ONEIRIS v0.6.

### 3. Experimental Design & Decisive Primary Protocol

#### 3.1 Four-Role Triple-Blind Architecture
- **Role A (Air-Gapped Target Custodian / Server, $\ge 50\text{ m}$ away):** Draws $T_i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ via hardware QRNG XOR NIST Beacon; computes $H_{T,i} = \text{SHA256}(\text{trial\_id}_i \parallel \text{pool\_id}_i \parallel T_i \parallel \nu_i)$ via `toolkit/commit_reveal.js` and logs it to an append-only hash-chained ledger. Blind to PSG, dreamer identity, and responses.
- **Role B (PSG Operator):** Monitors 64-ch EEG, bilateral EOG (`LOC`/`ROC`), submental EMG, and automated blinded TTS prompt triggers. **Strictly blind to $T_i$ and candidate pool $\mathcal{P}_i$; never speaks live during REM.**
- **Role C (Participant):** Experienced lucid dreamer screened for reliable `LRLR x3` signaling and REM stability; sleeps inside a Faraday ($\ge 60\text{ dB}$ RF) and acoustic ($\ge 55\text{ dB}$) shielded suite with $40\text{ dB}$ pink-noise masking and blackout mask.
- **Role D (Automated Classifier + Blind AASM Scorers):** Two AASM-certified scorers ($\kappa \ge 0.85$) verify $S_{\text{neuro}}$ and lock `PSG_lock_sha256` *before* target reveal.

#### 3.2 Trial Timeline (Primary Arm — Real-Time Lucid-REM Bit Transfer)
1. **State Lock ($S_{\text{neuro}} = S_1$):** Automated PSG confirms $\ge 180\text{ s}$ stable Stage R + `LRLR x3` lucidity signal + sub-awakening acoustic/TMS evoked-alpha reduction audit.
2. **Target Exposure:** Role A activates $T_i$ inside the physically isolated Target Room (25% of trials are randomly interleaved `Sham-Empty` or `Wake-Control` calibrations).
3. **Query & Intra-REM Signaling:** Blinded server delivers a neutral, non-semantic query tone ($\le 45\text{–}50\text{ dB}$ SPL, arousal-gated). While still in verified REM, the dreamer queries the dream environment (or a dream character in ECHO Step 4 sub-arm) and transmits their 4-choice response $R_i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ via standardized `LRLR` EOG pulse count (verified by automated classifier: $\ge \pm 75\ \mu\text{V}$, phase-opposed LOC/ROC, EMG atonia, calibrated to $q_{\text{FA}} \le 1\%$ on $25\%$ silent sham epochs).
4. **Cryptographic Lock & Reveal:** `commitResponse` hashes $(R_i, \text{audio\_sha256}, \text{PSG\_lock\_sha256})$ onto the append-only `TrialLedger` *before* Role A reveals $(T_i, \nu_i)$.
5. **Secondary Temporal Control (Exp 02):** In a dedicated leakage-control arm, `RESPONSE_COMMIT` is logged and externally timestamped $\ge 60\text{ s}$ *prior* to `TARGET_COMMIT` (enforced by `TrialLedger.verifyExp02TemporalOrder`).

### 4. Sampling Plan & Verified Statistical Analysis Plan

- **Primary Endpoint:** Exact Rank-1 / 4-choice Hit Rate ($p_0 = 0.25$) in verified $S_{\text{REM-Lucid}}$ trials.
- **Co-Primary Information-Theoretic Endpoint:** Conditional Mutual Information $I(T; R \mid S_{\text{neuro}})$ estimated via `toolkit/cmi_estimator.js` using Miller–Madow and Bayesian Dirichlet-smoothed estimators paired with $B = 10{,}000$ **within-stratum conditional permutations**.
- **Multiple-Comparison Control:** Holm–Bonferroni step-down across the 4 confirmatory THRESHOLD/ECHO arms at $\alpha_{\text{FWER}} = 0.005$ ($\alpha_{(1)} = 0.00125$).
- **Fixed-$N$ vs. Sequential BFDA Power (Verified):**
  - *Fixed-$N$ Confirmatory:* $N = 311\text{–}388$ active trials per arm provides $80.9\%\text{–}90.6\%$ power for $p_1 = 0.35$ at $\alpha = 0.00125$ (reaching $K = 1{,}000$ pooled multi-lab trials for the decisive adversarial benchmark where $\ge 32.5\%$ yields $>5.4\sigma, p < 10^{-6}$).
  - *Sequential BFDA (`toolkit/bfda_sequential.js`):* $H_0: p = 0.25$ vs. $H_1: p \sim \text{Truncated-Beta}(4.5, 8.5)$ on $(0.25, 1]$, $N_{\min} = 60$, $N_{\max} = 400$, step $= 10$, boundaries $BF_{10} \ge 30$ (confirm) vs. $BF_{10} \le 0.10$ (falsify), maintaining $< 1\%$ false-positive rate under $H_0$ and $> 93\%$ power at $p_1 = 0.35$.
