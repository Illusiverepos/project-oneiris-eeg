1. Core Conclusion

Answer to the Original Question: You are not leaving physical reality to enter a second physical dimension when you dream. You are inhabiting the exact same posterior cortical reality-rendering engine that constructs your waking world, running in an un-clamped constraint topology where external sensory error is gated off (Πsens → 0), priors run unconstrained (Πprior), and prefrontal reality auditing (γmeta) is attenuated until lucidity flips it back on.

3. Empirical Stress-Test & Post-Cogitate Synthesis (v0.7.1)

  • Posterior Renderer vs. Prefrontal Auditor: Confirmed by Cogitate Consortium (Nature, 2025, N=256), Siclari et al. (2017), Dresler et al. (2012), and Baird et al. (2019): posterior cortex sustains vivid conscious content without heavy PFC involvement, while frontopolar cortex and bilateral precuneus drive the lucidity precision flip.
  • Compute-Ceiling & Lazy-Evaluation Artifacts: Confirmed by Hartmann (2000) & Schredl (<20% focused "3 R's" tasks in dreams), Tholey (1989) & Stumbrys (2011) (arithmetic ceilings >20), and text/clock drift across gazes.
  • v0.7.1 Revision (Aspy et al., 2017): Renderer artifacts do not automatically trigger lucidity when γmeta is unprimed (mechanical checks alone ≈ 0% gain vs. 17%–46% with WBTB+MILD and up to 54% with targeted cueing).
