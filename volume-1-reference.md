## Frozen Hypothesis Evolution
- **Original premise (Phase I):** REM sleep opens a doorway into another dimension. Broadened in Phases I–II because dreaming occurs outside REM (citing 2017 Nature Neuroscience posterior cortical hot zone).
- **Models (Phase I):** Model A (Internal generation), Model B (External access), Model C (Hybrid access). Conceptual equation: `D = f(M, E, N, S) + αX` (M=Memory, E=Emotional state, N=Neurological activity, S=Ordinary sensory info, X=Hypothetical external info, α=Hypothetical degree of external access).
- **v0.3 (Phase III):** "Certain neurological states may permit conscious access to information originating outside conventional sensory and memory processes. If this information originates from an observer-independent domain, it should exhibit measurable properties of persistence, consistency and shared accessibility." (Information access separated from dimensional claim.)
- **v0.4 (Phase IV):** Four postulates: (1) Independent domain, (2) Neurological coupling, (3) Perceptual translation, (4) Empirical detectability. Gateway equation: `G = α(1 - S)CV` (S=ordinary sensory dominance, C=conscious awareness, V=capacity for conscious perceptual experiences, α=coupling to independent domain, G=proposed accessibility; all normalized 0..1). Interactive default example: S=15%, C=85%, V=90%, α=0% -> G=0.00%.
- **v0.5 (Frozen after Phase VIII):**
  - *Established background:* "Certain neurological states permit immersive conscious experiences that are substantially disconnected from ordinary sensory perception."
  - *Speculative proposition:* "We hypothesize that some of these states might also permit access to independently existing information, a proposition that requires controlled experimental verification."

- **Phase V Frameworks & Metrics:**
  - **State-Dependent Reality Coupling (SDRC)** (p. 29): Hypothetical component proposing consciousness remains associated with the brain while information accessible to it varies by neurological state (change in perceptual accessibility, not physical travel).
  - **Oneiric Physics** (p. 29): Hypothetical discipline examining measurable laws (space, time, matter, causality, identity, information) governing dream environments.
  - **Physics Frameworks Audited (p. 28):** Holographic principle, emergent spacetime (Van Raamsdonk, 2010), extra spatial dimensions (CERN), many-worlds interpretation, quantum consciousness (Orch OR + Tegmark decoherence), and quantum no-communication theorem.
  - **Phase V Metric:** Conditional mutual information `I(T; R | S)` where T=randomly selected hidden target, R=recorded dream response, S=verified neurological state; null hypothesis `I(T; R | S) = 0`.

## Four Proposed Research Branches
1. **THRESHOLD (Phase III):**
   - Exp 01 (Spatial access): Identify randomly selected object in physically inaccessible room.
   - Exp 02 (Temporal access): Identify randomized target selected only after awakening.
   - Exp 03 (Shared access): Two isolated dreamers exchange specific information without conventional communication.
   - Exp 04 (Dimensional persistence): Changes made to a dream environment independently discovered by another dreamer later.
   - Simulator example: 100 trials, 4 equally likely targets (25% chance), 40 hits (40.0%), p = 0.068659%.
   - Phase V Information-Theoretic Metric: Conditional mutual information `I(T; R | S)` where T=randomly selected hidden target, R=recorded dream response, S=verified neurological state; null hypothesis `I(T; R | S) = 0`.
2. **ATLAS (Phase VI):**
   - Dream cartography & spatial persistence (spatial continuity, temporal continuity, observer independence, discoverability).
   - Prototype **Dream Reality Index** (not validated): Spatial consistency 70/100, Environmental persistence 50/100, Predictable navigation 60/100, Independent observer agreement 0/100, Verifiable hidden information 0/100 -> Environmental consistency 60%, Independent information score 0%.
   - **Undiscovered Room Experiment:** Computer generates virtual building with 16 rooms, each containing a randomly assigned object; participant sees only exterior, attempts to enter in dream, reports before seeing interior.
3. **ECHO (Phase VII):**
   - Dream character autonomy (Neurological simulation vs Partitioned cognition vs Independent agency).
   - Extends real-time lucid dream dialogue (Konkoly et al., 2021) + five test categories (creative writing, math problems, forgotten memories, random hidden targets, info exchange between isolated dreamers).
   - **Independence Ladder:** (1) Unexpected behavior, (2) Creative performance, (3) Consistent personality, (4) Verified inaccessible information, (5) Replicated independent interaction.
4. **LIMINAL (Phase VIII):**
   - Cross-state boundary study across Ordinary waking, Hypnagogia, REM, NREM, Sleep paralysis, Certain anesthetic states, Psychedelic states (DMT), NDEs, OBEs.
   - Key neurological lead: Reduced evoked alpha power as a marker of sensory disconnection (2018 paper).
