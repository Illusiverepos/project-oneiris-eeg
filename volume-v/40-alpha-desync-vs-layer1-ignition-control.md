## 1. The Video Control Catch (Why Controls Save Science)

In Report 39, both **Lucid Dream Control** (`TWC_USA`, `p = 0.00195`) and **19 mg Psilocybin** (`ds006110`, `p = 0.0050`) drove **Layer-1 `slopeBB`/`LZc` UP** while driving **`crossIrrevPost` DOWN**. To test whether `crossIrrevPost` collapse reflected *sensory disconnection* or *visual/experiential alpha desynchronization*, we ran a 4-condition within-subject control across the exact same **18 paired humans** in OpenNeuro `ds006110` (64-ch 10–10 Hjorth CSD):

1. **Sober Resting** (`ses-01`)
2. **Sober Guided Meditation** (`ses-01`, zero drugs)
3. **Sober External Video** (`ses-01`, maximal external visual drive)
4. **19 mg Psilocybin Guided Meditation** (`ses-02`)

### Within-Subject Results (`n = 18` Paired Humans, 10,000 Sign-Flip Permutations)

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sober Guided Meditation vs. Resting** | 12/18 (`+0.155`, `p = 0.53`) | 9/18 (`+0.318`, `p = 0.20`) | 10/18 (`-0.037`, `p = 0.88`) | **7/18 pos (`d_z = -0.558`, `p = 0.0281`) ↓** |

### Physical Interpretation
- **External Video collapsed `crossIrrevPost` in 17 / 18 humans (`d_z = -1.147, p < 0.0001`)**—the *strongest* drop of any condition!
- That proves **`crossIrrevPost` in eyes-closed sober resting is driven by idling posterior Alpha travelling waves** (which disappear the instant the visual/spatial cortex actively renders a scene—whether from an **external movie**, a **19 mg Psilocybin vision**, **Sober Guided Meditation**, or a **Lucid Dream**).
- Meanwhile, **Psilocybin Meditation vs. Sober Meditation** still produced a massive **`Right_TPJ` + `Occipital` 1/f (`p = 0.0006–0.0052`) and `LZc` (`p = 0.0103`) surge**—proving eyes-closed psychedelic immersion genuinely activates the **exact same posterior visual-spatial rendering engine** as watching an external movie (`Video` vs. `Resting` `Right_TPJ` `slopeBB` `p = 0.0114`, `LZc` `p = 0.0004`) *without any light entering the eyes*.

## 2. Post-Cue Agency Test in Lucid REM (`TWC_USA`, `n = 20` Paired Recordings)

Comparing **Responded Cues** (verified `R-HEOG` eye-signal reply in `[+1..+10 s]`) vs. **Missed Cues** inside the exact same REM recordings (`276` cues total):
- In the quiet post-signal window (`[+10..+16 s]`, strictly after eye movements end), **Posterior (`Right_TPJ` + `Occipital`) `slopeBB`** was significantly higher after **Responded vs. Missed cues** (**14 / 20 recordings, `d_z = +0.593, p = 0.0133`**).
- Thus, **`Right_Frontal` 1/f** tracks the **block-level executive control mode** (`11/12` humans, `p = 0.019`, Report 39), while **Posterior `slopeBB`** tracks **trial-by-trial sustained lucid engagement** after an active response (`p = 0.0133`).
