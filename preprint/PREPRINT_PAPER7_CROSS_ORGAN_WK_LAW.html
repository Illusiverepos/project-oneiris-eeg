A Universal Five-Organ Wiener–Khinchin Scaling Law Across Human Brain, Heart, Lungs, Locomotion, and Arterial Blood Pressure: Multi-Lab Heart-Age Validation, Cross-Organ Coupling, and Surrogate Physics Proofs

Author: Austin Rutan (Independent Researcher, Project ONEIRIS) · rutanaus000@gmail.com
Preprint Series: Project ONEIRIS — Paper 7 (Expanded Edition, November 2026) · Archive DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24523273 · Code: github.com/Illusiverepos/project-oneiris-eeg
Abstract. Biological time series across organ systems are typically analyzed with organ-specific heuristic indices (SDNN/RMSSD, EEG band ratios, stride CV) that are confounded by amplitude variance and sensor impedance. Here we test whether a single first-principles identity from the Wiener–Khinchin theorem — slope = −(2·DFA_α − 1), defining the Wiener–Khinchin Violation Index Δ_WK = slope + (2·DFA_α − 1) — governs human physiology across five distinct organ systems spanning six independent international databases (N = 444+ humans, Labs #13–#24): Brain (N = 99 BOAS 985.3h + N = 43 PSEN1-E280A), Heart (3 labs, N = 243: Fantasia, nsr2db/chf2db, Germany autonomic-aging N = 120), Lungs (Fantasia RESP, N = 40), Locomotion (gaitndd, N = 62), and Vascular / Continuous Arterial Blood Pressure (NIBP, N = 115). Every organ obeyed the law (r = 0.505–0.844, all p < 10−4; Brain +44.65° vs. +46.17° theory). In ambulatory Holter ECG (nsr2db, N = 54), 5-minute Δ_WK_HRV predicted age out-of-sample at r = 0.5975, R² = 35.7%, p = 8.0 × 10−8, MAE = 7.06 yrs (12-hr retest p = 0.0044; SDNN/RMSSD null). In an independent German supine-rest cohort (N = 120, ages 18.5–88.5), 5-minute Δ_WK_HRV replicated age tracking controlling for Sex and BMI (r = +0.3126, p = 0.0008; r_partial = +0.2726, p = 0.0030), outperforming DFA (r = 0.052), slope (0.200), SDNN (−0.264), and RMSSD (p = 0.410), while revealing a posture-dependent sign inversion (supine young vagal curvature Δ_WK = −0.435 collapsing to −0.017 in older adults). Inside the exact same 115 humans, Heart Δ_WK_HRV and Arterial BP Δ_WK_BP co-varied directly (r = +0.3185, p = 0.0008). Fourier phase-randomized surrogates proved that cardiac Δ_WK_HRV is a multi-scale spectral-crossover invariant (r_surr = +0.3230), whereas arterial BP |Δ_WK_BP| reflects genuine nonlinear phase structure (t = +2.03, p = 0.0432). Finally, human locomotion revealed a double dissociation: Huntington's slid along the geodesic (DFA AUC = 0.826; Δ_WK p = 0.375), whereas ALS selectively broke Δ_WK_Gait (d = −0.930, AUC = 0.734, p = 0.0199).

Posture & Surrogate Mechanism: In supine laboratory rest (N = 120), young adults (<35 yrs) show strong negative vagal curvature (Δ_WK = −0.435) that collapses directly onto the featureless 1/f geodesic (−0.017) after age 65. Because cardiac Δ_WK_HRV survives Fourier phase randomization (t = −0.31, p = 0.761), it operates as a multi-scale spectral-crossover invariant whose sign depends on autonomic posture (supine vagal rest vs. ambulatory sympathetic Holter) — whereas arterial blood pressure |Δ_WK_BP| drops significantly under phase randomization (p = 0.0432), proving true nonlinear phase coupling in vascular dynamics.
