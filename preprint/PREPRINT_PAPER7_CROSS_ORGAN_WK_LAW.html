A Universal Cross-Organ Wiener–Khinchin Scaling Law Across Human Brain, Heart, Respiration, and Locomotion: 5-Minute Smartwatch Heart-Age Clock and Multi-System Validation

Author: Austin Rutan (Independent Researcher, Project ONEIRIS) · rutanaus000@gmail.com
Preprint Series: Project ONEIRIS — Paper 7 (November 2026) · Archive DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24523273 · Code: github.com/Illusiverepos/project-oneiris-eeg
Abstract. Biological time series across different organ systems are typically analyzed with organ-specific, heuristic metrics (SDNN/RMSSD for heart rate, band ratios for EEG, stride CV for gait) that are confounded by amplitude and sensor impedance. Here we test whether a single first-principles identity derived from the Wiener–Khinchin theorem — slope = −(2·DFA_α − 1), defining the Wiener–Khinchin Violation Index Δ_WK = slope + (2·DFA_α − 1) — governs human physiology across four distinct organ systems (Brain, Heart, Lungs/Respiration, and Locomotion/Gait; Labs #13–#23, N = 324+ humans). Every organ obeyed the law: Brain (+44.65° vs. +46.17° theory across 985.3h), Heart (r = +0.7166, p < 10−7 Fantasia; r = +0.657 nsr2db/chf2db), Lungs (r = +0.7762, R² = 60.3%, p < 10−8), and Locomotion (r = +0.8444, R² = 71.3%, p < 10−4). Crucially, Δ_WK tracked biological aging with the same negative sign in both Brain (REM Δ_WK r_partial = −0.2969, p = 0.0022 vs. raw slope p = 0.966) and Heart (nsr2db, N = 54: r = −0.6099, R² = 37.2%, p = 1.0 × 10−6 vs. null SDNN p = 0.233 and RMSSD p = 0.314). From just 5 minutes (360 heartbeats), a 1-feature smartwatch clock (scoreHeartWK) achieved strictly out-of-sample 5-fold CV r = 0.5975, R² = 35.7%, p = 8.0 × 10−8, MAE = 7.06 years with 12-hour same-human retest stability (r = +0.3671, p = 0.0044). Respiration aging was null (p = 0.306), and locomotion showed a double dissociation: Huntington's Disease slid along the geodesic (DFA d = −1.524, p = 0.0002, AUC = 0.826; Δ_WK p = 0.375), whereas ALS selectively broke Δ_WK_Gait (d = −0.930, AUC = 0.734, p = 0.0199) while sparing DFA and Slope alone.

4. Zero-Dependency SDK Implementation & Research Notice

The 5-minute Heart-Age Clock is shipped as scoreHeartWK(rrIntervalsSec) inside sdk/oneiris_wearable_sdk.js (zero external dependencies; 5/5 automated self-tests passing in sdk/demo_sdk.js). Reproducible scripts: audit_lab20_fantasia_heart_wk_transfer.js, audit_lab21_physionet_chf_vs_nsr_heart_wk.js, audit_lab22_cross_organ_smartwatch_and_respiration.js, and audit_lab23_gaitndd_locomotion_wk.js.

Non-Clinical Research Notice: Project ONEIRIS software and SDK modules are open-source non-commercial research and general-wellness prototypes, not FDA-cleared medical devices or clinical diagnostic substitutes.
