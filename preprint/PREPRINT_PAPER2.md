## Abstract

In Paper 1 (7 labs, 1,291 human EEG recordings), we showed that natural NREM N2 dreaming is generated by two-stage 6-second posterior ignition waves localized to the **Right Temporo-Parietal Junction (`Right_TPJ`) and Occipital Cortex**, while **Right Prefrontal Cortex (`Right_Frontal`) remains `0/3` offline**, and that a frozen 3-parameter propofol-trained physical axis ($S_{\text{phys}} = +0.515\,z(\text{slopeBB}) + 0.351\,z(\text{LZc}) - 0.782\,z(\text{DFA }\alpha)$) transfers zero-shot across four unseen sleep labs ($67/90$, $p = 1.76 \times 10^{-6}$). Here, we extend this identical reference-free Hjorth Surface Laplacian (CSD) framework to **three additional independent laboratories (Labs #8–#10)** spanning real-time two-way lucid dream communication (`TWC_USA`, Konkoly et al., 2021), 58–116-channel lucid HD-EEG (`Donders`), and 64-channel 19 mg Psilocybin vs. sober resting, meditation, music, and external video (`OpenNeuro ds006110`, $n = 56$ complete 5-state paired humans), alongside `22,315` DreamBank narratives.

We report six converging discoveries:
1. **The 16-Second Right-Prefrontal Dream-Control Switch (Lab #8, `TWC_USA`):** After EOG micro-saccade window rejection and Local Hjorth CSD, active two-way dream communication selectively flips **`Right_Frontal` (`F4`/`F8`) 1/f `slopeBB` ON** in **11 / 12 unique humans ($d_z = +0.750$, exact $p = 0.0190$)** and **14 / 17 sessions ($p = 0.0097$)**—locking ON across **7 consecutive windows (`+6..+22 s`, peak `+12..+16 s` $d_z = +1.174, p = 0.0029$)** before cleanly returning to baseline (`+22..+26 s`, $p = 0.607$, ruling out time-in-REM drift), while mirror **`Left_Frontal`** remains null ($p = 0.294$). Post-signal **Posterior `slopeBB`** further separates responded vs. missed cues ($14/20, d_z = +0.593, p = 0.0133$). Directionally replicated in Lab #9 (`Donders` 58–116-ch HD-EEG).
2. **"Inner Cinema" Exceeds "Outer Cinema" & 96% Identical Cortical Geometry (Lab #10, `ds006110`, $n = 56$ Paired Humans):** Directly comparing **19 mg Psilocybin eyes-closed rest** (zero light) against **sober external video** (full-color movie) inside the same 56 humans, both share **96.0% identical 6-ROI 1/f geometry ($\cos = 0.9604$)** and cross-decode zero-shot (**Video $\to$ Psilocybin: $45/56, 80.4\%, p < 0.0001$**; **Psilocybin $\to$ Video: $46/56, 82.1\%, p < 0.0001$**), while eyes-closed Psilocybin *exceeds* live external video in **`Parietal_Midline` ($38/56, d_z = +0.420, p = 0.0037$)** and **`Occipital` ($34/56, d_z = +0.281, p = 0.0381$)**. Body-weight dose (`0.156–0.352 mg/kg`) scales selectively with **`Right_TPJ` $\Delta$1/f ($\rho = +0.300, p = 0.0222$, #1 ROI in the brain)**.
3. **The 2D Coordinate System of Human Reality (`S_phys` $\times$ `R_tether`):** Applying Paper 1's frozen propofol equation zero-shot across `ds006110` ($n = 56$) places **19 mg Psilocybin eyes-closed rest ($+0.356$) significantly above both sober rest ($-0.220, p = 0.0014$) and live external video ($-0.109, 37/56, p = 0.0293$)**. Crucially, a **zero-parameter Reality Tether equation** ($R_{\text{tether}} = +z(\text{effDim}_{\text{post}}) + z(\text{DFA }\alpha) - z(\log\text{Beta}_{13\text{--}25\text{ Hz}})$) separates **External Reality (Video)** from **Internal Simulation (Psilocybin)** in **$45/56$ humans ($80.4\%, d_z = +0.727, p < 0.0001, \text{AUC} = 0.755$)**. An **Eyes-Open vs. Eyes-Open stress test** (`Psilocybin_Video` vs. `Sober_Video`, $n = 56$, `logAlpha` identical $p = 0.551$) proves that even while staring at the exact same movie, 19 mg Psilocybin simultaneously elevates `slopeBB` ($36/56, p = 0.0001$) and *reduces* retinal spatial dimensionality (`effDimPost` $d_z = -0.278, p = 0.0422$; `R_tether` $d_z = -0.265$, one-sided $p = 0.0257$).
4. **Temporal Phase Diagram & Sober Music `burstP90` Waves ($n = 56$):** Both External Video ($45/56, p < 0.0001$) and Psilocybin Rest ($36/56, p = 0.0271$) collapse `metastabilityGap` (`burstP90 - tonicP10`), locking Layer 1 into a continuous tonic floor. Conversely, **sober eyes-closed music** selectively triggers transient dream-like **`burstP90` posterior ignitions ($36/56, d_z = +0.387, p = 0.0063$, zero drugs)**, and **Psilocybin + Music** produces the highest `Right_TPJ` 1/f flattening in the entire 10-lab archive ($48/56, d_z = +0.873, p < 0.0001$) while restoring dynamic wave metastability ($35/56, p = 0.0147$).
5. **Cross-Referenced Individual Laws & 5-for-5 Right-Hemisphere Dominance:** Across 56 humans, Sober Video `Right_TPJ` predicts Psilocybin `Right_TPJ` ($\rho = +0.367, p = 0.0052$, shared visual hardware), `Right_TPJ` and `Right_Frontal` gains co-ignite ($\rho = +0.363, p = 0.0046$), and steeper baseline 1/f yields larger ignition jumps ($\rho = -0.403, p = 0.0030$, E/I ceiling law). Across all 5 cortical-topography paradigms, the **Right Hemisphere wins 5 / 5 times**, remaining the universal phenomenal stage **even inside verbal-dominant dreams** (`Zhang & Wamsley`, $n = 85$: `Right_TPJ` $\rho = +0.307, p = 0.0050$ vs. `Left_TPJ` $\rho = +0.006, p = 0.955$).
6. **Empirical Philosophy of Dreaming & Closed-Loop Engineering:** Decomposing `Siclari 2017` NREM N2 ($n = 287$) reveals that contentless minimal awareness (`CEWR` / "white dreams") completes **81% of the `DFA α` drop** before **0% of `slopeBB` flattening** occurs. Across `22,315` DreamBank dreams, **bodily sensory prediction errors** ("pillow hands" / sleep leaks) bypass conceptual doubt ($\text{OR} = 1.38\times$, n.s.) and trigger full lucidity directly ($\text{OR} = 8.77\times, \chi^2 = 69.3, p < 10^{-15}$). Finally, we implement and validate a real-time **Goldilocks Closed-Loop TLR Cueing Engine** (`closed_loop_engine.js` + `tlr_trainer.html`) incorporating `DFA α` early-warning gating, `WAKE_GUARD` backoff, World-Seed sensory incorporation, and embodied haptic cueing.

## 1. Introduction

Paper 1 resolved the core neurophysiology of disconnected non-lucid dreaming across seven laboratories, isolating a **Right TPJ + Occipital** Layer-1 engine operating in 6-second ignition waves while **Right Prefrontal Cortex** stayed silent. Three fundamental questions remained:
1. **Executive Control Inside Sleep:** When a dreamer becomes lucid and actively steers or communicates from inside REM sleep, what exact cortical switch turns back on—and why is dream control so notoriously fragile?
2. **Psychedelic "Inner Cinema" vs. External Vision:** Does an eyes-closed psychedelic state run on the same Layer-1 posterior engine as dreaming and external vision—and where does it sit on Paper 1's frozen propofol consciousness axis?
3. **Empirical Philosophy & Real-Time Translation:** Can our 10-lab biophysical framework empirically constrain classical philosophy-of-dreaming debates (Dennett, Block, Metzinger, Thompson, Windt, Revonsuo, Bayne) and translate directly into a closed-loop dream-control cueing architecture?

## 2. Results Summary (Reports 39–45)

### 2.1 The 16-Second Right-Prefrontal Dream-Control Switch (Labs #8 & #9)
In `TWC_USA` ($n = 12$ unique humans, $17$ paired sessions, 21-ch Local Hjorth CSD with `R-HEOG` micro-saccade window rejection), active two-way dream communication (`Status` codes `1–20` vs. pre-cue baseline sleep) flipped **`Right_Frontal` (`F4`/`F8`) 1/f `slopeBB` ON** ($11/12$ humans, $d_z = +0.750, p = 0.0190$; $14/17$ sessions, $p = 0.0097$) while mirror **`Left_Frontal`** stayed null ($6/12, p = 0.294$). Time-resolved `[-10..+30 s]` curves locked to prompt onset showed **Posterior** leading (`+2..+6 s`, $d_z = +0.654, p = 0.0117$), followed by a **16-second sustained `Right_Frontal` plateau (`+6..+22 s`, 7 consecutive bins $p < 0.032$, peaking at `+12..+16 s` with $10/12$ humans, $d_z = +1.174, p = 0.0029$)**. In Lab #9 (`Donders`, 58–116-ch HD-EEG), post-LRLR lucid execution replicated the exact directional pattern (Posterior UP $4/5$, `Right_Frontal` UP $3/5$, `crossIrrevGlobal` DOWN $4/5$). An extended Two-Layer Wilson–Cowan model (`biophysical_two_layer_lucid_obe_model.js`) reproduced a bounded **inverted-U lucidity corridor (`gFP = 1.5–2.5`)**: insufficient top-down gain leaves the dream non-lucid, whereas excessive gain breaches the waking threshold.

### 2.2 Inner Cinema Exceeds Outer Cinema & The Universal Propofol Axis (Lab #10, $n = 56$)
Across `56` complete 5-state paired humans in `OpenNeuro ds006110` (64-ch 10–10 Hjorth CSD):
- **Inner > Outer Cinema:** Eyes-closed 19 mg Psilocybin significantly exceeded live external Video in `Parietal_Midline` ($38/56, d_z = +0.420, p = 0.0037$) and `Occipital` ($34/56, d_z = +0.281, p = 0.0381$).
- **#1 Dose-Response Hub:** Exact `dose_mg_per_kg` tracked $\Delta$1/f `slopeBB` most strongly in **`Right_TPJ` ($\rho = +0.300, p = 0.0222$)**, followed by `Left_TPJ` ($\rho = +0.265, p = 0.0462$), while frontal ROIs were null ($p = 0.16$).
- **Control Catch (`crossIrrevPost`):** External Video collapsed `crossIrrevPost` in $17/18$ humans ($d_z = -1.147, p < 0.0001$), proving that `crossIrrevPost` in sober eyes-closed rest reflects **idling posterior Alpha travelling waves** that desynchronize whenever Layer 1 actively renders a scene (Movie, Psilocybin, Meditation, or Lucid Dream).
- **Super-Waking Frozen `S_phys`:** Paper 1's frozen propofol equation ordered all 5 states with **Psilocybin Rest (`+0.356`) > Psilocybin Med (`+0.197`) > External Video (`-0.109`) > Sober Rest (`-0.220`)**, beating live video in $37/56$ humans ($p = 0.0293$).
- **Temporal Phase Diagram & Sober Music:** Both Video ($p < 0.0001$) and Psilocybin Rest ($p = 0.0271$) locked into continuous tonic floors (`metastabilityGap` ↓), whereas **sober eyes-closed music** selectively triggered transient dream-like `burstP90` waves ($36/56, p = 0.0063$, zero drugs) and **Psilocybin + Music** hit peak `Right_TPJ` flattening ($48/56, d_z = +0.873, p < 0.0001$) with restored wave metastability ($p = 0.0147$).

### 2.3 Cross-Referenced Laws & Empirical Philosophy of Dreaming
- **Individual Laws ($n = 56$):** Sober Video `Right_TPJ` predicts Psilocybin `Right_TPJ` ($\rho = +0.367, p = 0.0052$); `Right_TPJ` and `Right_Frontal` co-ignite ($\rho = +0.363, p = 0.0046$); steeper sober baseline yields larger Psilocybin gain ($\rho = -0.403, p = 0.0030$).
- **5-for-5 Right-Hemisphere Invariant:** The Right Hemisphere dominated across all 5 cortical-topography labs—and in `Zhang & Wamsley` 58-ch CSD, **`Right_TPJ` remained the universal phenomenal stage even inside verbal-dominant dreams ($n = 85, \rho = +0.307, p = 0.0050$ vs. `Left_TPJ` $\rho = +0.006, p = 0.955$)**.
- **Minimal Phenomenal Experience (`CEWR`):** In `Siclari 2017` ($n = 287$), `CEWR` ("white dreams") completed **81% of the `DFA α` drop** (`1.0727 → 1.0550`) with **0% of `slopeBB` flattening** (`-1.4403 → -1.4415`), dissociating temporal release from broadband world-rendering.
- **Embodied Self-Model Shattering (`DreamBank`, $N = 22,315$):** Bodily prediction errors ("pillow hands" / sensory leaks) bypassed conceptual doubt ($\text{OR} = 1.38\times$, n.s.) and triggered full lucidity directly ($\text{OR} = 8.77\times, \chi^2 = 69.3, p < 10^{-15}$).
