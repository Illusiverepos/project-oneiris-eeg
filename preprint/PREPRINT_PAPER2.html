Abstract

How does the human brain distinguish externally tethered reality from an internally generated simulation, and what cortical switch enables executive agency inside a dream? Extending our seven-lab reference-free Hjorth Surface Laplacian (CSD) consciousness pipeline to three additional laboratories (Labs #8–#10)—real-time two-way lucid dream communication (TWC_USA, Konkoly et al., 2021), 58–116-channel lucid HD-EEG (Donders), and 64-channel 19 mg psilocybin versus sober resting, meditation, music, and external video (OpenNeuro ds006110, n = 56 complete 5-state paired humans)—plus 22,315 DreamBank reports, we uncover a three-axis physical architecture of human reality:

  • The 16-Second Right-Prefrontal Dream-Control Switch (Lab #8, TWC_USA): After EOG micro-saccade window rejection and Local Hjorth CSD, active two-way dream communication selectively switches Right Prefrontal (F4/F8) 1/f slopeBB ON (11/12 unique humans, dz = +0.750, exact p = 0.0190; 14/17 sessions, p = 0.0097)—locking ON across 7 consecutive windows (+6..+22 s, peak +12..+16 s dz = +1.174, p = 0.0029) before cleanly returning to baseline (+22..+26 s, p = 0.607, ruling out time-in-REM drift), while mirror Left Frontal stays null (p = 0.294). Directionally replicated in Lab #9 (Donders).
  • "Inner Cinema" Exceeds "Outer Cinema" & 96% Identical Cortical Geometry (Lab #10, n = 56): Eyes-closed 19 mg psilocybin (zero light) and live external video share 96.0% identical 6-ROI 1/f geometry (cos = 0.9604) and cross-decode zero-shot (Video → Psilocybin: 45/56, 80.4%, p < 0.0001; Psilocybin → Video: 46/56, 82.1%, p < 0.0001), with eyes-closed psilocybin exceeding live video in Parietal_Midline (38/56, p = 0.0037), Occipital (34/56, p = 0.0381), and our frozen propofol consciousness axis S_phys (37/56, p = 0.0293), and showing a #1 dose-response in Right_TPJ (ρ = +0.300, p = 0.0222).
  • The Zero-Parameter Reality Tether Equation (R_tether): A zero-training equation, R_tether = +z(effDim_post) + z(DFA α) − z(logBeta_13-25Hz), separates External Reality (Video) from Internal Simulation (Psilocybin) in 45/56 humans (80.4%, dz = +0.727, p < 0.0001, AUC = 0.755) and survives a strict Eyes-Open vs. Eyes-Open video control (Psilocybin_Video vs. Sober_Video, n = 56: logAlpha identical p = 0.551; slopeBB ↑ p = 0.0001; effDim_post ↓ p = 0.0422; R_tether ↓ one-sided p = 0.0257).
  • Temporal Phase Diagram & Sober Music burstP90 Waves (n = 56): Both External Video (p < 0.0001) and Psilocybin Rest (p = 0.0271) collapse metastabilityGap into a continuous tonic floor, whereas sober eyes-closed music selectively triggers dream-like burstP90 posterior ignitions (36/56, p = 0.0063, zero drugs).
  • Empirical Philosophy of Dreaming & 5-for-5 Right-Hemisphere Invariant: Across all 5 cortical-topography labs, the Right Hemisphere dominates 5/5 times, remaining the universal phenomenal stage even inside verbal-dominant dreams (Zhang & Wamsley, n = 85: Right_TPJ ρ = +0.307, p = 0.0050 vs. Left_TPJ ρ = +0.006, p = 0.955). In Siclari 2017 (n = 287 NREM N2), contentless awareness (CEWR) completes 81% of the DFA α drop before 0% of slopeBB flattening. Across 22,315 DreamBank reports, bodily prediction errors ("pillow hands" / sleep leaks) bypass conceptual doubt (OR = 1.38×, n.s.) and trigger full lucidity directly (OR = 8.77×, χ² = 69.3, p < 10−15).

1. Introduction

In Paper 1 (7 independent laboratories, 1,291 human EEG recordings), we demonstrated that natural NREM N2 dreaming is generated by two-stage 6-second posterior ignition waves localized to the Right Temporo-Parietal Junction (Right_TPJ) and Occipital Cortex, while Right Prefrontal Cortex remains 0/3 offline, and that a frozen 3-parameter propofol-trained physical axis (S_phys) transfers zero-shot across four unseen sleep labs (67/90 paired humans, p = 1.76 × 10−6). Here, we address three fundamental follow-up questions: (1) what exact cortical switch turns back on when a sleeper gains executive control inside a lucid dream, (2) how the brain distinguishes an internally generated "Inner Cinema" from live external vision when both share the same posterior rendering hardware, and (3) how these findings constrain classical debates in the philosophy of dreaming and translate into closed-loop dream-control engineering.

2. Results

2.1 The 16-Second Right-Prefrontal Dream-Control Switch (Labs #8 & #9)

In Lab #8 (TWC_USA, Konkoly et al., 2021; 21-ch 10–20 Local Hjorth CSD, 1000 Hz → 200 Hz, EOG micro-saccade windows > 4.5 MAD rejected), active two-way dream communication (Status math prompts 1–20 vs. pre-cue baseline sleep) selectively flattened Right_Frontal (F4/F8) 1/f slopeBB in 11/12 unique humans (dz = +0.750, exact p = 0.0190) and 14/17 sessions (dz = +0.686, p = 0.0097), while mirror Left_Frontal (F3/F7) remained null (6/12, p = 0.294). Time-resolved [-10..+30 s] curves locked to prompt onset (t = 0) revealed Posterior leading (+2..+6 s, dz = +0.654, p = 0.0117), followed by a 16-second sustained Right_Frontal plateau across 7 consecutive windows (+6..+22 s, all p < 0.032; peak +12..+16 s: 10/12 humans, dz = +1.174, exact p = 0.0029), before returning cleanly to baseline at +22..+26 s (p = 0.607)—ruling out time-in-REM wake drift. Post-signal Posterior slopeBB (+10..+16 s) further separated HEOG-responded vs. missed cues (14/20 recordings, dz = +0.593, p = 0.0133). Lab #9 (Donders, 58–116-ch HD-EEG) directionally replicated Posterior ↑ (4/5), Right_Frontal ↑ (3/5), and crossIrrevGlobal ↓ (4/5). Extending our 12-column Wilson–Cowan E/I sheet with a coupled prefrontal module reproduced a bounded inverted-U lucidity corridor (g_FP = 1.5–2.5).

2.3 Temporal Phase Diagram, Sober Music Bursts & Individual Laws (n = 56)

  • Tonic Floor vs. Pulsed Bursts: Both External Video (45/56, p < 0.0001) and Psilocybin Rest (36/56, p = 0.0271) collapsed metastabilityGap (burstP90 − tonicP10) into a continuous tonic floor, whereas sober eyes-closed music selectively elevated transient dream-like burstP90 waves (36/56, dz = +0.387, p = 0.0063, zero drugs). Psilocybin + Music produced the peak Right_TPJ flattening of the 10-lab archive (48/56, dz = +0.873, p < 0.0001) while restoring dynamic wave metastability (35/56, p = 0.0147).
  • Cross-Referenced Individual Laws: Sober Video Right_TPJ predicts Psilocybin Right_TPJ (ρ = +0.367, p = 0.0052, shared visual hardware); Right_TPJ and Right_Frontal gains co-ignite (ρ = +0.363, p = 0.0046); and steeper sober baseline 1/f yields larger ignition jumps (ρ = −0.403, p = 0.0030, E/I ceiling law).

2.4 Empirical Philosophy of Dreaming & 5-for-5 Right-Hemisphere Invariant

  • Minimal Phenomenal Experience (CEWR): In Siclari 2017 (n = 287 NREM N2), white dreams (CEWR) completed 81% of the DFA α drop (1.0727 → 1.0550) before 0% of slopeBB flattening (−1.4403 → −1.4415), dissociating temporal slow-wave release from broadband world-rendering.
  • Embodied Self-Model Shattering (22,315 DreamBank Dreams): Bodily prediction errors ("pillow hands" / sleep sensory leaks) bypassed conceptual doubt (OR = 1.38×, n.s.) and triggered full lucidity directly (OR = 8.77×, χ² = 69.3, p < 10−15), vs. 3.39× for pre-lucid doubt and 2.06× for threat/nightmares.
  • Universal Right-Hemisphere Stage: Across all 5 cortical-topography paradigms, the Right Hemisphere won 5/5 times—and in Zhang & Wamsley 58-ch CSD, Right_TPJ tracked narrative richness even inside verbal-dominant dreams (n = 85, ρ = +0.307, p = 0.0050) while mirror Left_TPJ remained zero (ρ = +0.006, p = 0.955).

3. Discussion & Closed-Loop Translation

Together, Sphys (Engine) × Rtether (External Anchor) × Right_Frontal (Steering Wheel) define a 3-axis physical coordinate system of human reality. Waking perception is a Right_TPJ world-simulation tethered to external detail (effDim), external time (DFA), and a live Right_Frontal reality-checker; dreaming is the same simulation untethered.
