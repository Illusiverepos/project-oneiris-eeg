A Universal +50° Cortical Plasticity Vector Across 12 Human EEG Labs, Wearable Theta wPLI Remission Coupling, and Closed-Loop Benchmarking of Sleep & Dual-Dream Interfaces

Abstract. Synthesizing 12 independent human EEG laboratories (Papers 1–3, 1,400+ recordings) onto a Complete 2×2 (slopeBB × DFA α) Phase Plane reveals a Universal +50° ± 4° Cortical Plasticity Vector across four countries and four unrelated human domains with zero parameter tuning: waking from propofol anesthesia (+47.3°, Lab #1), active fear extinction (+46.6°, Lab #12), clinical PTSD remission (+54.2°, Lab #11), and transitioning from external video into psychedelic Inner Cinema (+54.6°, Lab #10) all execute an invariant ΔDFA α ≈ −0.071 release (−0.0691, −0.0698, −0.0740). Conversely, external video (−36.7°) and fear conditioning (−68.0°) move nearly perpendicular (~90° orthogonal) into locked Quadrant Q3. In Lab #12 (n = 28), the zero-parameter composite E_extinct = +z(ΔpostSlope) − z(ΔpostDFA) achieves 20/28 consistency (10,000-shuffle permutation p = 0.0032). Evaluating four new wearable metrics identifies volume-conduction-free Theta wPLI (4–8 Hz, Posterior ↔ Right_Frontal) re-coupling as a specific biomarker of clinical PTSD remission (+0.121, dz = +0.712, p = 0.0244, 8/10; MI control p = 0.752), alongside Posterior Higuchi Fractal Dimension (postHFD) surging in 12/14 lucid REM humans (sign p = 0.0129), while ruling out spectral curvature as non-specific session drift. Auditing N = 20,897 DreamBank reports confirms zero passive couple convergence (0.77×) while spatial architecture (13.55%) transfers 6×–12× better across minds than nature or emotion. Finally, benchmarking our live 4-channel controller across 160 simulated sessions yields 100.0% safe REM precision and 0.00 accidental wake-startles in PTSD irt-tmr mode (vs. 6.92 startles/session for naive timers) and 84.4% safe delivery with 0.000 partner startles for a 20-minute store-and-forward Dual-Dream Baton relay (1.92 bits/min LRLR capacity).

3. Cross-Person Dream Convergence & 160-Session Closed-Loop Benchmark

Auditing N = 20,897 DreamBank reports (10,000 sampled pairs/cohort) showed zero passive dream convergence in a real married couple (emma × emmas_husband: 0.77× vs. strangers), whereas Spatial/Architectural Places (13.55%) transferred 2.2×–12.4× more reliably than social figures (6.18%), emotions (1.74%), or nature (1.09%). A 10,000-night REM Monte Carlo showed 38.8 min/night unsynchronized overlap (69.6 min/night with 5h WBTB sync) and 99.9%–100% deliverability using a 20-minute store-and-forward Dream Baton (1.92 bits/min LRLR capacity; coupled Wilson–Cowan relay r = +0.71 to +0.81 at gRelay = 0.30–0.40).

Limitations & Scope: Closed-loop benchmarks used synthetic spectral states and warrant prospective overnight validation against clinical PSG (e.g., OpenNeuro ds007052 BOAS). All software tools (including the 4-channel Muse 2/S Web Bluetooth adapter) are non-commercial open-source research prototypes, not medical devices. Hardware-mediated dream conjoining is strictly sensory/relay-based (~1.92 bits/min) and requires blind matched vs. mismatched pilot trials (n ≥ 7 nights for 80% power at 3× lift).
