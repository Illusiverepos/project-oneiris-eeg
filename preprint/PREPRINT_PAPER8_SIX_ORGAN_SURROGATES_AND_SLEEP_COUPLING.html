Linear Spectral Curvature vs. Nonlinear Phase Coupling Across Human Sleep, Dreaming, Aging, and Neurodegeneration: A Multi-Organ Fourier Surrogate Decomposition (N = 411)

Author: Austin Rutan (Independent Researcher, Project ONEIRIS) · rutanaus000@gmail.com
Preprint Series: Project ONEIRIS — Paper 8 (Final Unified Edition, November 2026) · Prior DOIs: 10.5281/zenodo.24599665 (P5), 24599906 (P6), 24604316 (P7)
Abstract. Any empirical departure from the stationary Wiener–Khinchin scaling law (Δ_WK = slope + 2·DFA_α − 1) can arise either from linear multi-scale spectral-crossover curvature or genuine nonlinear phase coupling. Here we separate these two mechanisms across seven independent international cohorts (N = 411 humans; Labs #24, #25, #30–#34) using exact power-spectrum-preserving Fourier phase-randomized surrogates (Theiler et al., 1992). Four unifying discoveries emerge: (1) The Brain–Heart Sleep Phase-Switching Mirror Law (capslpdb, N = 31 paired full nights): during Deep NREM (S3/S4), the Heart is strongly nonlinear phase-coupled (t = −2.973, p = 0.0050, 24/31) while the Brain is linear Gaussian (p = 0.239); during REM, they invert — the Brain locks into nonlinear phase coupling (t = −3.254, p = 0.0012, 22/31) while the Heart relaxes to linear Gaussian (p = 0.219), causing the cardiac Deep NREM→REM Δ_WK shift (p = 0.0321) to collapse to null in surrogates (p = 0.568). (2) Age-Invariant Prodromal Dreaming Biomarker in RBD: in REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (14 RBD vs. 9 Healthy, Lab #33), raw brainSlope (AUC=0.556), brainDFA (0.587), and brainWK (0.563) all failed, yet pure nonlinear cortical phase (brainNonlin = Real − Surr) jumped to AUC = 0.921, d = −1.624, p = 0.0010 (−0.412 vs. −0.032) alongside REM heartDFA (AUC = 0.821, p = 0.0239); an independent 64-year healthy lifespan control (BOAS, 22 Young 18–26 vs. 22 Older 55–82, Lab #34) proved healthy REM brainNonlin is age-invariant (−0.110 vs. −0.163, AUC = 0.537, p = 0.366). (3) Nonlinear Phase Destruction in FTD & ALS: in FTD (ds004504, N = 88), pure nonlinear phase separated FTD from Controls (d = +0.609, p = 0.0269; surrogate null p = 0.097); in ALS gait (gaitndd, N = 62), real Δ_WK (d = −0.930, p = 0.0202) collapsed to null in surrogates (p = 0.413). (4) Linear Spectral-Crossover Flattening: pre-symptomatic PSEN1-E280A AD (N = 43; 77% surrogate survival p = 0.041) and supine cardiac aging (N = 120; 100% surrogate survival) operate primarily via linear spectral-crossover flattening toward 0.00, while arterial BP (N = 115) exhibits genuine nonlinear phase coupling (p = 0.0432).
