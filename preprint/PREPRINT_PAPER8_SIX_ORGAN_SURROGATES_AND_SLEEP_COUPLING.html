Six-Organ Wiener–Khinchin Scaling, Nonlinear Surrogate Proofs in Pre-Symptomatic Alzheimer's, and Synchronized Brain–Heart–Vascular Sleep Dynamics

Author: Austin Rutan (Independent Researcher, Project ONEIRIS) · rutanaus000@gmail.com
Preprint Series: Project ONEIRIS — Paper 8 (November 2026) · Prior DOIs: 10.5281/zenodo.24599665 (P5), 24599906 (P6), 24604316 (P7)
Abstract. Extending our five-organ Wiener–Khinchin framework (Paper 7, 10.5281/zenodo.24604316) across Project ONEIRIS Labs #25–#29, we verify the universal law slope = −(2·DFA_α − 1) in a sixth human organ system — Skeletal Muscle (EMG) — and test four mechanistic predictions: (1) Skeletal Muscle (emgdb, 4000 Hz, 153 windows) obeyed the Wiener–Khinchin law at r = +0.9482 (R² = 89.9%, p < 10−4) with a monotonic collapse from Healthy (Δ_WK = −0.122) → Myopathy (−0.090) → Neuropathy (+0.010); (2) Fourier phase-randomized surrogates in pre-symptomatic PSEN1-E280A Alzheimer's (ds007427, N = 43) confirmed a real-vs-surrogate cortical phase offset (t = −2.004, p = 0.0451) and proved that early amyloid pathology drives 77% spectral-crossover flattening toward 0.00 (−0.530 → −0.210, matching supine heart aging −0.435 → −0.017) + 23% nonlinear phase boost (real d = +0.931, p = 0.0065 vs. surrogate d = +0.718, p = 0.0410); (3) Simultaneous 4-organ overnight sleep (slpdb, N = 18) replicated within-human Heart–Arterial BP Δ_WK coupling, nearly doubling in sleep (r = +0.6120, R² = 37.5%, p = 0.00570) with a positive Brain–Heart trend (r = +0.434, p = 0.072); (4) across clinical sleep stages (slpdb .st), cardiac Δ_WK_HRV shifted significantly from Deep NREM (−0.710) to REM (−0.234, dz = −1.196, p = 0.0486, 6/7 humans) in lockstep with Brain Δ_WK (p = 0.033), where Heart DFA (p = 0.139) and Heart Slope (p = 0.362) failed; and (5) in CHB-MIT Epilepsy (N = 24), 2-channel forehead inter-hemispheric 1/f slope asymmetry |slope_L − slope_R| spiked during active seizures in 17/24 patients (dz = +0.471, t = +2.309, p = 0.02095) while pre-ictal asymmetry remained honestly null (p = 0.25–0.99).
