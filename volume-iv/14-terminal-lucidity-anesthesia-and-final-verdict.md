## 2. Anesthesia Dreaming & The Isolated Forearm Technique (Sanders et al., 2012)

  3. **Unconsciousness:** Absence of subjective experience (deep propofol, dreamless N3).

## 3. Retrospective Veridical NDEs (Pam Reynolds) vs. Prospective Trials (AWARE II)

Why do famous retrospective anecdotes look like undeniable proof of out-of-body perception while prospective controlled studies return zero visual hits?

  2. **Sensory Leakage via Bone Conduction:** Molded BAER ear speakers attenuate ambient air sound but do **not** create acoustic isolation; high-frequency skull-saw vibrations transmit directly through cranial bone to the inner ear.
  3. **Anesthesia Connected Consciousness:** As Sanders et al.'s (2012) IFT review later established (median 37% connected consciousness under anesthesia), brief intraoperative sensory awareness followed by cognitive visual reconstruction is a well-documented physiological mechanism.
- **The Prospective Contrast:** When researchers eliminate retrospective timeline ambiguity and bone-conduction leakage using **sealed, hidden visual targets** (AWARE II, Parnia et al., 2023; 567 cardiac arrests, 28 interviewed), objective visual target identification drops to **0 out of 28 (0%)**.
